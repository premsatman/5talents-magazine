# The Philippines — 50 candidates

Researched 9 September 2026 across five parallel searches: musicians, authors and public figures, pastors and ministry leaders, NGO and social enterprise, theological faculty.

**Rule applied throughout, and it did most of the work.** The Philippines is roughly 90 per cent Christian, so being a Filipino Christian is not evidence of anything. Every person below is classified by whether faith actually shapes the work. Nobody was included for nationality, upbringing, or thanking God in an awards speech. Emails were only recorded where a researcher read them on a page they opened; anything weaker says so.

**Two names appear in two categories**, so the true count of distinct people is 48: Lee Simon Brown and Harald Huyssen both surfaced under musicians and again under public figures.
