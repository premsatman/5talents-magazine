# Philippines — the first ten sends

Every address below was read on a real page. Nothing guessed.

Ten letters reaching twelve people. Seven commissions, one publisher introduction covering three authors, one column approach, one interview.

**Why these ten.** They are the only Philippine contacts with a verified email and operative faith evidence. Everyone else needs a DM, a form or an intermediary, which is a separate evening's work.

**Every commission is deliberately small, safe and specific.** That is the Mohan Chacko lesson: our first Indian commission asked a retired principal to write about what had gone wrong in the church over forty years, and he declined politely. Too large, too exposed, from a magazine he had never heard of.

They rely entirely on someone else telling them. I would like a scholar to teach that skill in about a thousand words, using one passage, so that a reader can do it again the following week without us.

What we offer: your byline, a light edit that you approve before it runs, and links to whatever you would like linked. There is no fee. We do not pay contributors and we do not charge them.

Anything by the end of November would be ideal, though I would rather have it late than rushed.
