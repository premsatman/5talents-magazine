## The letter

**Subject:** An interview about the shop — 5Talents Magazine, India

Dear Jes,

I am Premasis Satman, editor of 5Talents Magazine, a Christian magazine published
from Hyderabad, India since 2012. The archive is at 5talentsmag.com and the monthly
issues resume in January 2027.

You wrote a line under one of your posts that I have not been able to put down:
"Using my gifts to glorify Him in the smallest details."

Our magazine is named for the parable of the talents, so I should tell you that line
is more or less our whole editorial position, written better and shorter than we
have ever managed it.

Our readers are Christians in their twenties and thirties, most of them in India,
with a good number in the United States, the Philippines, Canada and the Gulf. A
great many of them have a thing they are good at and have been told, quietly, that
it is a hobby and not a living. You said on your shop page that you hope the work is
"a conversation starter that may lead someone to Salvation." I think for our readers
it would start a different conversation, and one they badly need to have.

But I do not want a piece about clay. I want the part people never show.

What I would ask about is the shop. You opened your page at the end of December last
year with four lines and no orders. Nineteen hundred sales later, every one made,
sanded, polished, packed and posted by one person. Why you moved to limited monthly
drops instead of keeping the shop open. What happens when the orders come in faster
than your hands. How you decide what a charm is worth when the hours are yours. Why
you chose a printer in Longmont for the stickers when it would have been cheaper not
to. For us a business is not a footnote to the faith, it is where the faith is
happening.

How we work. Ten questions sent ahead, answered in writing, at whatever length suits
you. Nothing live, nothing on camera. No fee in either direction, we do not pay for
interviews and we do not charge for coverage. You see the piece before it is
published and you can cut anything in it. Two or three high resolution photographs
would help, credited to whoever took them.

If you are open to it, reply and I will send the questions.

With respect,
Premasis Satman
Executive Director, 5Talents Magazine
5talentsmag.com
