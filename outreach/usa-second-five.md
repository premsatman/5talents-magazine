I am Premasis Satman, editor of 5Talents Magazine, a Christian magazine published from Hyderabad, India. We ran from 2012 to 2014, stopped, and relaunch in January 2027. The archive is at 5talentsmag.com.

I imagine you receive a great many requests for the same interview, so let me say what would be different about this one.

I am not asking you to tell the story of the industry again. I would like to ask what you have learned in twelve years of ministry about how people actually get free, and what you would say to a twenty-two-year-old in a country where nobody will say the word out loud.
