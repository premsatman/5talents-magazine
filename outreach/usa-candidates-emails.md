## Sending notes

- Send 1–9 first (Monday 7 Sep), 10–15 midweek, 16–20 the following week. Don't send more than five a day from one address; Gmail treats a burst of near-identical emails as spam.
- Log each send with a date. One follow-up after three weeks, then stop.
- Photos, embeds and question sheets come after a yes, not before.
- The Indian-heritage voices (13, 14) are the ones most likely to say yes quickly. If either does, ask them for two more names.
