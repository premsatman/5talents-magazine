## 1. Fr. Dr Ignatius Oko — Bigard Memorial Seminary, Nigeria

**To:** ohajuoko@bigardenugu.org
**Cc:** ohajuoko@hotmail.com
**Subject:** A commission for Fr. Dr Ignatius Oko — 5Talents Magazine, India

Dear Fr. Oko,

I am Premasis Satman, editor of 5Talents Magazine, a Christian magazine published from Hyderabad, India. We ran from 2012 to 2017, stopped, and relaunch in January 2027. The archive is at 5talentsmag.com.

I would like to commission one piece from you, and I have chosen the subject carefully because I have seen what you write for Torch.

Take one hard saying of Jesus, whichever one you find people stumble over most, and walk a twenty-two-year-old through reading it in context. Not a survey of hard sayings. One. Show the working: what the passage seems to say at first, what is around it, what changes when you see that, and where an honest reader is still left uncomfortable.

The reason I want this from a Nigerian Catholic priest rather than from someone here is that our readers have almost never been taught how to read a difficult passage. They have been told what it means. When it does not sit right, they assume the fault is in them and quietly stop asking. Nobody has shown them that the asking is the work.

About a thousand words. Your byline, a light edit that you approve before it runs, and links wherever you would like them. No fee in either direction, and we do not charge anyone for coverage.

End of November would suit us, later if that suits you better.

If this goes well for both of us, I would like to talk about doing it monthly. But I would rather you saw one piece through first and decided for yourself.

Why we are back after a long pause of our own: https://www.5talentsmag.com/faith/why-we-stopped-in-2014-and-why-we-are-back

With respect,
Premasis Satman

## 4. Rev. Dr Kevin Muriithi Ndereba — St Paul's University, Kenya

**To:** kndereba@spu.ac.ke
**Subject:** A commission on doubt — 5Talents Magazine, India

Dear Dr Ndereba,

I am Premasis Satman, editor of 5Talents Magazine, a Christian magazine published from Hyderabad, India. We ran from 2012 to 2017 and relaunch in January 2027. The archive is at 5talentsmag.com.

I would like to commission one piece, and it is the piece I most want in the January issue.

How do you have one honest conversation with a friend who tells you they have stopped believing?

Not apologetics. Not a method for winning. One conversation, the kind that happens in a hostel room or a car park at eleven at night, where the person in front of you is somebody you love and you can feel yourself about to say something stupid. What to ask. What to leave alone. What to do with the silence. What it costs you to not have an answer ready.

This is happening constantly among young Indian Christians and nobody writes about it. The friend leaves quietly, the other one panics and argues, and the friendship is the thing that breaks. Your work on youth ministry and apologetics for a Gen Z African readership is the closest I have found to somebody who has actually thought this through rather than issuing instructions.

About a thousand words. Your byline, a light edit you approve, links wherever you would like them. No fee either way. End of November.

I will be direct: of everyone I am writing to across Africa, you are the person I would most like to carry a monthly column. One piece first, and then let us see.
