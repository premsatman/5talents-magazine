# Bill & Elveera Redwood — interview, 2027

**Status:** said yes 8 September 2026 on WhatsApp. Elveera: "Sure! But we will take out time to get it done. Not in a hurry."
**Deadline given:** 15 November 2026, offered as a guide not a deadline. December is fine.
**Format:** joint interview. They may answer together or separately. Where they remember differently, that is the best material in the piece — print both.
**Section:** Former contributor

## A. The covering note

Dear Bill and Elveera,

Thank you for saying yes, and for saying you will take your time. That suits me better than a quick answer would.

Twelve questions are below. Some are for both of you, some are addressed to one or the other — those are marked. Answer together or separately, whichever is easier. Where you remember something differently, please both say so rather than settling on one version; the disagreements will be the most interesting part of the piece.

Written answers, at whatever length suits. Voice notes are fine too if that is easier, and we will transcribe. 15 November was a guide rather than a deadline. December is fine. Tell me roughly when to expect them and I will leave you alone until then.

You keep copyright. We publish first, edit lightly, and you both read and approve the piece before anything runs. Anything either of you wants taken out comes out, and neither of you needs to give me a reason.

Two of the questions ask about your time at 5Talents and about me. Please be honest in them. I will state plainly in the published piece that you ran this magazine, so readers know the relationship before they read a word of it, and I would rather print something uncomfortable than something polite.

Photographs would help a great deal — Dubai, the India years, and something recent. Old prints are perfectly fine, we can scan them. Tell me who took them where you can.

Bill, how are you keeping? I saw a prayer bulletin from a few years back asking about your back. That is old news by now and I hope it has settled, but I did not want to write to you both and not ask.

With love and respect,
Premasis

## B. The twelve questions

**1. Both, and please answer this one separately.** PAOC describes your journey as having taken you full circle — from your birth nation, to Canada, and back. Where was each of you born, and what was the household like? Was faith there from the start, or did it arrive later?

**2. Both.** Before Canada there was Dubai — Elveera teaching English, Bill at a newspaper agency. What took you there, and what was it like being Christians in that place, at that time?

**3. Both.** Somebody said it first. Which of you raised the idea of going to India as workers, and what did the other one say that day?

**4. Separately, please, and without conferring.** What do you think the other one gave up to do this?

**5. Both.** You did not arrive in India as foreigners. You went home. Does the word *missionary* even fit when it is your own country? Did people there treat you as one of them, or as something else?

**6. Elveera.** Conversational English, resume writing, interview skills. Of all the things you could have taught rural young people, you chose those. Why? What did a nineteen-year-old walk into your room not knowing how to do?

**7. Bill.** You ran in-service training for business staff in vision, planning and marketing. Most Christians would not call that mission work; they would call it a job. Did you call it mission work, and how did you explain it to people who expected something more obviously spiritual?

**8. Both.** You also worked with street people and with the children of rag-pickers. Is there one person from those years you still think about?

**9. Elveera.** In 2013 you became editor-in-chief of this magazine. Tell the truth: what state was it in when you arrived, and what did you actually have to fix? I was twenty-two and I am fairly sure I did not know what I was doing.

**10. Bill.** Stephen Devassy on the first anniversary cover was your doing. How did you get him? And what did you understand about that magazine's chances that I did not?

**11. Both.** I was a young man who would rather have sat at a desk than walked into a room, and the two of you kept pushing me into rooms and onto stages at business conferences. Was that a deliberate decision about me, or had you simply run out of other people to send?

**12. Both.** You gave India about ten years and then went home to Canada. How does a person know when a thing is finished rather than merely hard? And what do you miss?

## Notes for the editor

- **The disclosure is not optional.** They were the editor-in-chief and the advertising manager of this magazine. A reader who discovers that halfway through has been misled. One plain line near the top, in the same register as the Basanti piece.
- **Questions 9 and 11 are the spine, and the temptation will be to soften them in the edit.** Do not. You have asked two people who mentored you at twenty-two to say what they actually thought of you and of the magazine. If Elveera says the magazine was a mess, print that it was a mess. A former editor being candid about the publication she is appearing in is the thing that makes this piece worth reading rather than a tribute.
- **Question 4 is the one to protect.** Asked separately and without conferring, it is the question most likely to produce two genuinely different answers. If they send a single joint document, write back and ask for that one twice.
- **Question 3 likewise.** Couples tend to have converged on an agreed version of who decided what. Push gently for the original disagreement.
- **Bill's health stays in the covering note and out of the piece.** The Peel Pentecostal bulletin asking prayer for his back is from November 2022 and is four years stale. Ask kindly, once, in the private note. Do not make it a question and do not publish it unless he raises it himself.
- **Do not raise Peel Pentecostal Tabernacle's South Asian fellowship.** It runs a 4pm South Asian service in Brampton and is a natural readership for us abroad, but an ask now would make the interview look like a means to something else. Do the interview, let it go well, and let them offer. Revisit only after publication.
- **Do not chase on 15 November.** Elveera said they would take their time and we agreed. A nudge in early December is the earliest that is fair.
- **Facts to confirm with them, not to assume.** Their birth nation — PAOC says "birth nation" without naming it and we should not fill that in ourselves. The exact years in India, and whether "about ten" is right. Whether Shiloh Global Solutions still trades. The spelling of Elveera. Which PAOC designation they prefer in print — "global workers" is PAOC's own term.
- **Their own framing is faith at work**, which sits directly alongside Paul Balasundaram's column. Worth placing the two pieces in the same issue if the timing allows.
- **Photographs:** Dubai, the India years, recent. Old prints fine.
- **Identification question is closed.** The November 2022 Peel bulletin names them in full and names Hyderabad, while anonymising another worker in a restricted-access nation in the same document. PAOC chose not to anonymise them. Name them normally.
