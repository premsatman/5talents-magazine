# Sound Barrier — plan for the piece, the Instagram post, and the reply

**Everything is in hand.** Ten answers, eleven photographs, two logos, figures cleared for print. Nothing is blocking this except writing it.
**Deadline that matters:** live well before **31 October**. Earlier is better — it is a registration driver, not a review.

---

## 1. The angle

**You cannot get into this conference without walking through a job fair.**

That is literally true, and Gwyneth gave it to us without realising what she had handed over:

> "From the registration desk, our entry into the conference is being designed in a way that the attendees walk through the trade show into the job fair and then into the stadium... Exit is also going to be designed in a way where the people will walk out through the trade show and job fair."

Nobody can skip it. You arrive for worship and a keynote, and the building makes you pass employers and founders on the way in and on the way out again.

That is the whole argument 5Talents exists to make — that a person's work and their faith are not two separate files — built into the floor plan of a stadium. It is the thing no other write-up of this conference will notice, and it is why this is a piece rather than a listing.

**Second strongest thing, and the proof:** the Industry Leaders section runs *during lunch*. Leaders are already at their tables when the room empties; you collect your plate and walk to whichever table you want. Mentoring with your food in your hand. And:

> "Last year we had one of the social media volunteers receive a job offer from one of the businesses."

**The pull quote, which is almost perfect as written:**

> "Walk in with all your questions and not just your resume."

### Headline options
1. **You can't get in without walking through the job fair** — best, concrete, slightly surprising
2. **Bring your questions, not just your résumé** — their line, safest
3. **The leadership conference you enter through a trade show**

### Standfirst
A one-day leadership conference in Hyderabad on 31 October has been designed so that nobody reaches the stage without passing a job fair first. ₹399, doors at 8:30.
