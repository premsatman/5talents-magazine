## 1. Lichan Humtsoe — Ete Coffee Roasters

**To:** etecoffee2016@gmail.com
**Subject:** Interview request from 5Talents Magazine, Hyderabad

Dear Lichan,

I am Premasis Satman, editor of 5Talents Magazine. We published from Hyderabad between 2012 and 2014, eighteen issues, and stopped when our daughter was born. The whole archive is now at 5talentsmag.com and we begin publishing again in January 2027. I would like to interview you.

What drew me was the name. Ete means ours. You could have built a roastery and put your own name on it, the way most people would, and instead you named it after everybody. Then you built a coffee school beside it and put a tenth of what you earn into training other people's children. That is a strange way to run a business and a very good way to run one.

Our readers are in their twenties, many of them in the northeast, and a great many of them are being told that ambition and service are opposite things. I do not think you believe that. I would like them to hear why.

I should be straightforward about one thing. This is a Christian magazine, and I would like to ask you what part faith plays in how you have built this, if it plays one. Not a set of expected answers. Whatever is actually true.

What I am asking for: ten questions, sent ahead, about an hour of your time. Answer in writing or by voice note, whichever is easier. You see the piece before it is published and we link whatever you would like linked. We would also be grateful for two or three high-resolution photographs, credited to whoever took them.

No fee either way. We do not pay for interviews and we do not charge for coverage.

A little on why we are back after a long pause of our own: https://www.5talentsmag.com/faith/why-we-stopped-in-2014-and-why-we-are-back

If you are open to it, reply here and I will send the questions.

Kind regards,
Premasis Satman
