# USA — 20 public conversions

Researched 9 September 2026, two parallel searches: actors and presenters, then musicians and industry exits. Template given by the editor: Brittni De La Mora, lovealwaysministries.com.

**Not one verified email was found in the entire twenty.** Every route below is Instagram, a ministry contact form, a speaking-booking page or an agent. That is normal for American public figures and it means this list is slower to work than the Philippine faculty, where six people had live addresses.

---

## The three decisions to make before sending anything

**1. Brian Welch first.** He built orphanages in India. Nobody else here has already invested in our readers' country, and it turns a celebrity profile into a story that belongs to us rather than one we borrowed.

**2. The adult-industry cluster.** Four of the twenty came out of pornography or OnlyFans. One, handled carefully, is a real piece. Four would define the magazine, in India, for twenty-year-olds. Choose one deliberately.

**3. The pattern worth noticing.** The cleanest stories here are not about leaving sin. They are about **leaving success**: Cameron Arnett turning down a role and going bankrupt, Montell Jordan walking away from a song that still plays at every wedding, Brian Welch leaving Korn at its height. Those travel to India better, carry almost no risk, and speak directly to a reader deciding whether to take the safe job.

## What to send first

1. **Brian Welch**, through MN2S. Lead with the orphanages, not with Korn. He has given to India already; we are the Indian magazine asking him why.
2. **Cameron Arnett**, through the Christ Over Career form. Lowest risk, and the question of turning down the role that would have made you is the best question on this list.
3. **Montell Jordan**, through Master Peace Church. A small self-run church is far likelier to answer than an agency.
4. **Crissy Moran**, through her site. Smallest platform, best odds, and a real story about the unglamorous years after.
5. **Melissa Joan Hart**, via World Vision. The lightest story here, and the one closest to our readers' actual experience: faith becoming real in your twenties without a crisis.

Then decide on the adult-industry piece, and choose ONE.

## Notes on method

- No email addresses were found for any of the twenty. Every route is a form, a DM, a booking page or an agent.

- Sensitive content is stated plainly on every row rather than softened, so the decision is made before commissioning rather than at edit.
