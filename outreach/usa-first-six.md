# USA — the first six approaches

All six go through a contact form, a booking agency or a DM. Not one has a verified email address, so these are deliberately shorter than the Philippine letters. Contact forms truncate, and agents stop reading after a screen.

Order matters. Send Welch first.

---

## 1. Brian "Head" Welch

**Route:** MN2S booking agency. Also DM @brianheadwelch in parallel, since agency forms disappear.
**Subject if the form has one:** Interview request from an Indian magazine — about Head Home

Dear colleagues,

I am writing to ask whether Brian Welch would consider an interview with 5Talents Magazine, a Christian magazine published from Hyderabad, India. We ran from 2012 to 2014 and relaunch in January 2027. The archive is at 5talentsmag.com.

We are not writing about Korn.

We are writing because of Head Home. He built orphanages in our country, housing and educating around two hundred children, and he did it long before it would have been a good story for anybody. We are the Indian magazine, and we would like to ask him why India.

Our readers are Indians in their twenties. Most of them have never heard of an American musician giving anything to this country without a camera present.

Ten questions, sent ahead, answered in writing or by voice note at his convenience. He sees the piece before it runs and can remove anything. We link whatever he would like linked. No fee in either direction.

Why we are back after a long pause of our own: https://www.5talentsmag.com/faith/why-we-stopped-in-2014-and-why-we-are-back

Kind regards,
Premasis Satman
Executive Director, 5Talents Magazine
5talentsmag.com

## Notes

- **Expect a low reply rate.** Every one of these goes through a form or a gatekeeper. In our own numbers, agent-routed approaches have the worst return of any channel. Crissy Moran has the smallest platform and therefore the best odds; Melissa Joan Hart and Brian Welch have the worst, and are worth trying anyway.
- **Send DMs in parallel with the forms** for Welch, Jordan, Hart and Moran. Forms vanish into inboxes nobody owns.
- **Follow-up: 30 September**, and then stop. Do not chase agencies twice.
- **The fourteen held back** are documented in usa-conversions-20.md with the reason for each. The one never to send is Bizzle.
