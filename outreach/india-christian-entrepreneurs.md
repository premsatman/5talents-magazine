# Christian entrepreneurs of India — top 20

Researched 5 September 2026, four parallel searches: South, North/West/Central, East/Northeast, and faith-driven ventures nationwide.

**Rule applied throughout:** nobody was included because of their name or their community. Every person here has a public source in which they, or their business, identify as Christian. Where that evidence is thin, it says so in capitals. Emails are only listed where the researcher read them on a page they actually opened; snippet-only addresses are flagged.

**Already on the contact list, not repeated here:** Humble Donkey (Secunderabad), Ruth's Café (Hyderabad).

## What the research says about the map

**The verifiable pool skews to music, media and apparel.** Those are the people who publish. Cafés, clinics, schools and manufacturers run by Christians exist in far greater numbers but leave no public trail of faith, which is itself a story: many keep quiet for exactly the reasons Humble Donkey and Ruth's Café decided not to.

**Nagaland is the richest single state on this list** — coffee, software, staffing, bakeries, disability employment, and an actual Christian entrepreneurship summit. Six of the twenty are from there.

**Nothing verifiable came back from** Mizoram, Kolkata, Jharkhand, Odisha, Bihar, Sikkim, Darjeeling, Punjab, Rajasthan, Gujarat, UP or Chandigarh. Kolkata's well-known Christian social enterprises (Freeset, Sari Bari, The Loyal Workshop) are foreign-founded. Go through the networks above rather than searching again.

**For the city-by-city plan later:** Nagaland and Chennai are ready now. Bengaluru and Mumbai need one intermediary each (LeadTalks, ICE). Delhi, Kerala and Hyderabad have two or three names apiece. The rest of the country needs a network introduction before it will yield anything.
