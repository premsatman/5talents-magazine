## C. The twelve questions

**1.** You found rap in 2012 through Honey Singh and Raftaar, while singing devotional songs at your Christian school. Those are two very different worlds. What was it about their music that caught you, and when did it first occur to you that you could do the same thing with the gospel?

**2.** Your father, Lambert Peter, has said he used to think, "How can the Lord be glorified through these things?" Do you remember the first time you played him something of yours? What did he actually say, and what did you feel?

**3.** What changed his mind? Was there a specific moment, or did it happen slowly?

**4.** People in your own church criticised it. Some thought rap meant sex, drugs and violence; some thought worship needed a choir or an acoustic set. What was it like to keep singing on a Sunday in front of people who had said that about you? Did anyone ever come back later and tell you they had been wrong?

**5.** You were told girls should not rap. Who said it, and what reason did they give?

**6.** "Yeshu Naam" came out in 2018, and you re-released it as "Kyun Darna" with a video in 2023 because people kept asking to see it. Five years is a long wait. What were you doing in between, and did you think anybody was listening?

**7.** You began serving in the slums at seventeen, where your parents were already working. Take us through what an ordinary day there actually looks like. Not the testimony — the ordinary part.

**8.** Is there one person from those streets you still think about?

**9.** On your YouTube videos you have been called anti-Indian and "rice-bag" Christians, and accused of converting people by force. You have said that at first the comments upset you and later you decided to stop reading them. I would like to ask about the part before you stopped reading them. What did those months do to you, and did it ever make you want to take the songs down?

**10.** You are twins who write together. Where do you disagree? Has either of you ever wanted to cut a line the other one loved?

**11.** You are both doing a master of divinity while running Hosanna El Shaddai and making music. Something has to give. What does, in practice — and how do you pay for any of it?

**12.** A girl of nineteen in Delhi has written a rap about Jesus on her phone and is afraid of what her church will say. She is reading this. What do you tell her?

## Notes for the editor

- **The spine is questions 2, 3 and 4.** The father's reversal is the piece. If his answers are good, consider asking Lambert Peter for two or three lines of his own to run as a sidebar — he is quotable and he is in Delhi, reachable through AG Prarthana Bhawan.
- **Question 9 is the one to handle carefully.** The "rice-bag" slur and the forced-conversion accusation are live legal and physical risks in India, not just hurt feelings. They have already talked about it in *Christianity Today*, so the subject is on the record and fair to raise, but get explicit permission before quoting them on it, and do not put a line in that could be read as a claim about any named person or group. If they decline, drop it; the piece survives without it.
- **Do not write the victory arc in advance.** Question 6 exists to find out what the five quiet years between 2018 and 2023 were actually like. That gap is probably the most useful thing in the interview for our readers, and it is the part every other profile has skipped.
- **Question 11 is the money question and they may not want to answer it.** Ask it plainly anyway. Joel Salvi told *Christianity Today* that rapping provides no fixed income; if the twins say the same, that is worth printing, because our readers keep being told to follow a calling without anyone explaining how it gets paid for.
- **Facts to confirm with them directly**, since all of this is secondhand: father's name and the church's exact name; whether the ministry is "Hosanna El Shaddai Ministry" or the "@hosannaministries07" name on Instagram; whether they have finished the MDiv by now; current YouTube subscriber count (*Christianity Today* had 13,000 in July 2025, their Instagram is at 90.3k, those two numbers are far apart); and whether "Lion of Judah" and the CBN India song about loneliness have been released.
- **Two YouTube channels**, "Varsha&Sherin" and "HosannaTwins" — ask which is the main one so we link the right one.
- **Sources used to build these questions:** *Christianity Today*, 30 July 2025; Rapzilla, 7 July 2025. Both are on the record and quotable, but everything attributed to them should be re-checked against what the twins tell us.
