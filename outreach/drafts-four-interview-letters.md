# NOTES FOR THE QUESTIONS STAGE — do not put these in the invitation

**Preacher Born Pathaw — the Yahuah question.** His channel About says his motto is to add listeners "to the Kingdom of Yahuah his God," and he signs off "Yah Bless you all." That is Sacred Name / Hebrew Roots vocabulary, not standard Khasi Presbyterian or Baptist usage. It may be nothing but personal devotional style. **Ask him what he means by it at the questions stage, gently and as a genuine question.** Do not print it uninterpreted, and do not raise it in a cold letter — you do not interrogate a stranger's theology in an invitation.

**Gianne Hinolan — do not call her a worship artist in the present tense.** Her entire music catalogue is about five years old, nothing since roughly 2021, and her channel About now reads "In her podcaster era!" Her current weekly output is a general-audience Tagalog lifestyle podcast, not a Christian show, though individual episodes are. She has also competed on secular television. The letter above is written around that reality rather than against it.

**KDB Michael — two things.** His site says born in Sundarbani, Jammu and Kashmir; his own video descriptions say he is from Churachandpur, Manipur. Both are probably true, but ask rather than print a guess. Separately, he is Paite, that is Kuki-Zo, and Churachandpur is the centre of the Manipur conflict. He has made **no political statement** anywhere that could be found. Decide in advance how much of that the magazine wants to touch, because for an India-read publication it will come up. Also: his own bio calls him "The Prolific Indian Christian Pop-Rock Sensation" and his studio "one of the best in the world." Edit that out rather than reprint it.

**Chi Bumanglag Santos — her website is stale.** The "upcoming releases" section is headed 2024, omits newer songs that are live on her channel, the Facebook link is dead, and the Facebook and Instagram labels are transposed. Trust nothing on that site except the email. Reach is genuinely tiny: 114 subscribers on the main channel, 169 on a second one.
