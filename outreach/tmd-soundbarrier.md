## Questions for the preview

Sent by email. Ordered so the interesting ones survive if they answer only the first few.

1. The job fair and the trade show sit in the same day as worship and ministry time. Was
   that deliberate from the start, or did it arrive later? What are you arguing?
2. Where did "Echo the past, sound the future" come from? What past, specifically?
3. Your site says everyone can speak but leadership requires more than volume. For a
   twenty-three-year-old with a phone and an opinion, what does that cost them to act on?
4. ₹399 for a full day in an indoor stadium is well below what this costs to run. What is
   the thinking, and what does it mean you have to say no to?
5. You have run The Table, Hurl, Pioneer Again. What did each teach you that changed how
   Sound Barrier is built?
6. Most Christian conferences in India are pastors talking to pastors. Yours puts a
   founder, a creative and a church team in the same room. Why?
7. What would make 31 October a failure, in your own terms?
8. What happens to the people in that room on 2 November? Is there anything after the day?

## Notes

1. **Pin a date for their account of the day.** A supplied report that has no deadline
   does not arrive. Ask for it within three days of the conference, while it is fresh and
   before everyone scatters — and say so when they agree to the preview, not afterwards.
2. **A supplied account is their version.** Write it up as reporting, attribute their
   figures to them, and check anything checkable against what appears publicly
   afterwards. This is the same discipline as the NBCC and Kairos reports.
3. **The site says timings may change** and more panellists may be announced. Re-check the
   lineup and running order the day before the preview publishes.
4. **Set `sponsorTier` to none on both pieces.** No money changes hands and we keep
   editorial control, so no disclosure label is required — but setting the field makes it
   a decision on the record rather than an omission.
5. **"Eighteen issues"** — the archive index lists nineteen. The other outreach letters
   already say eighteen, so this matches them, but settle the number and make it
   consistent before someone counts.
