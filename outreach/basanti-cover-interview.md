# Rev. Ms. M. G. Basanti — cover interview, January 2027

**To:** rev.basanti@gmail.com
**Language:** English
**Peg:** 35th anniversary of her ordination, 26 January 1992
**Disclosure required:** she is the editor's aunt. One plain line near the top of the published piece.

---

## Covering note

Dear Aunty,

I am writing to you as the editor of 5Talents, not as your nephew, though I know the two cannot be separated.

The magazine is coming back. The whole 2012 to 2014 archive is now online at 5talentsmag.com, and we begin publishing again in January 2027. You may not know that the very first issue, July 2012, is cited on your Wikipedia page. The first thing we ever printed helped put your name on the public record.

January 2027 will be thirty-five years since your ordination on 26 January 1992. I would like that issue to carry your story, and I would like it on the cover.

Not because you are my aunt. Because you were the first ordained woman in the Jeypore Evangelical Lutheran Church, because you spent eighteen years at the Kotpad boarding before that, and because of something you said in 2015 that I have never forgotten: that to your surprise, you never had a problem serving the Church. Most young people expect that story to be a struggle. Yours is not the story they expect, and that is exactly why they should read it.

I should say plainly that I will state our relationship in the article itself. Your record stands on its own, and I would rather our readers hear it from me than find it later.

Twelve questions are below. Answer in writing, in English, at whatever length you like. There is no hurry. Take a month, or take longer and tell me so. You will see the piece before it is printed and may remove anything you do not want in it.

One more thing. We would be very grateful for photographs, especially from the ordination in January 1992 and from the Kotpad years. Old prints are perfectly fine; we can have them scanned.

With love and respect,
Premasis

---

## The twelve questions

**1.** You were born in Shillong because your father was serving in the army, and you studied in schools across the country. What was that childhood like, and was there faith in the house, or did it come later?

**2.** In 1970 you went to Jensen Theological College at Kotpad as an aspirante. What made a young woman of twenty do that in those days? Did your family agree?

**3.** From 1974 you were matron of the girls' boarding at Kotpad, for eighteen years. Tell us what an ordinary day there was like. Who were those girls, and where did they come from?

**4.** Is there one girl from those years you still think about?

**5.** Eighteen years passed between finishing your training and your ordination. During those years, did you expect it would ever happen? What did you tell yourself?

**6.** Take us to 26 January 1992, in Jeypore. What do you remember of that day?

**7.** In 2015 you said something that surprises people: that to your surprise, you never had a problem serving the Church. Many assume the first woman in any church must have had a hard time. Why do you think it was not so for you?

**8.** Was there anyone who did not want it to happen? How did you deal with them?

**9.** Then seventeen years as pastor at Nabarangpur, until 2009. Most of that work nobody sees. What did those years actually consist of, week by week?

**10.** Was there a time you wanted to stop?

**11.** You said in 2015 that there were then eleven women pastors in your area, where once there was one. What changed, and who did the changing?

**12.** A young woman of twenty-two reads this. She believes God has called her, and the people around her are telling her to wait. What would you say to her?

---

## Notes for the editor

- Questions 7 and 8 are the spine of the piece. Ask 8 gently, but do ask it. If she says there was no opposition, that is the story. If there was, she has waited thirty-five years to be asked.
- Question 5 is where the emotional weight sits. Eighteen years is most of a career.
- Do not write the struggle narrative in advance. Let her answer 7 first.
- Photographs: 1992 ordination, Kotpad boarding years. A 1993 photograph of her exists in *Gurukul*, October 1993.
- Fact-check before printing: PAOC vs POAC is a separate matter, but confirm every date here against her own memory. Wikipedia has the ordination as 26 January 1992 and retirement as 2009.
