## B. The ten questions

**1.** In your own words, what is Sound Barrier, and what is the day for?

**2.** Where did the theme come from? "Echo the past. Sound the future," and the idea that before there was light, there was sound.

**3.** What do you want someone to walk out with at five o'clock that they did not walk in with at nine?

**4.** Who is this day designed for? Tell us the kinds of people you are hoping to see in the room.

**5.** Take us through the day. What actually happens between the doors opening and the close?

**6.** Tell us about your three speakers — Benjamin Komanapalli Jr., Justin Owens and Reinhard — and why you invited each of them.

**7.** Worship runs through the day rather than sitting at one end of it, and you have three worship leaders. What part do you want worship to play?

**8.** A Career and Job Fair and a Business and Trade Show are not what most people expect at a leadership conference. How do they work, and what should someone bring if they want to make use of them?

**9.** Tell us about *Collide*, the breakout sessions with industry leaders. What happens in those rooms?

**10.** Finally, the bigger picture: the story of The Mighty Dream so far, and what you are building towards.
