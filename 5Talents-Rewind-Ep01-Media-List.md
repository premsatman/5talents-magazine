## General rules for this edit
1. Every non-owned image gets an on-screen credit.
2. Representative stock images (baptism tub, stadium) are labelled so viewers don't think they show the actual event.
3. No song audio anywhere: Dusty Bibles, Oneness, worship clips. Use Epidemic Sound under your voice.
4. Show Instagram, X and Facebook posts as screenshots in a phone frame, on screen only while you talk about them.
5. Keep a note of each image's source next to the project, in case of a claim.
