**Purpose.** Find out whether the five-member editorial board requirement, and
in particular the institutional-affiliation part of it, applies to a
general-interest magazine — before spending six to eight weeks recruiting to a
standard that may not be the one they hold you to.

## Before you send

- **Send it from an address on the 5talentsmag.com domain.** Section 10 of the
  detailed information document treats the serial's primary mail ID as the
  channel of record for everything to do with the application. Starting that
  correspondence from a Gmail address, on a question about institutional email
  addresses, would not help.
- **Do not send it before the site is presentable.** They will open the URL. The
  editorial board page currently shows a holding line, which is honest and fine;
  a missing publisher address is not.
- **Save the reply.** If they confirm a lighter standard in writing, that reply
  is worth keeping with the application papers.
- **Expect it to take a while**, or to go unanswered. This is a question worth
  asking, not a gate to wait behind — keep recruiting in parallel on the
  assumption that the full requirement applies.

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am preparing an application for an online ISSN and would like to clarify one
point beforehand, so that what reaches you is complete.

The publication is 5Talents Magazine (https://5talentsmag.com), a
general-interest magazine on culture, work and personal formation, written for
young readers. It is not a scholarly or research journal: it publishes essays,
interviews and reviews rather than peer-reviewed papers. It was published in
print from Hyderabad between July 2012 and July 2014, running to eighteen
issues, and returned as a website in 2026. It is published by me personally;
there is no institutional publishing body.

My question concerns the requirement for a minimum of five editorial board
members, each with a complete institutional address, an institutional email
address and a profile page on the affiliated institution's website. I note that
the related requirement about senior faculty members is stated specifically for
scholarly journals, which is why I am unsure how the rest of the clause applies
in our case.

I would be grateful for your guidance on two points:

1. Does the minimum of five editorial board members apply in the same way to a
   general-interest magazine, or is a smaller editorial team acceptable where
   the publication is not a scholarly journal?

2. Where the requirement does apply, must every member hold an institutional
   affiliation? A magazine's editorial board is ordinarily made up of working
   editors and writers, many of them independent, who therefore have neither an
   institutional email address nor a profile page on an institution's website.
   If institutional affiliation is required I will recruit accordingly — I would
   simply rather know before approaching people than after.

I would prefer to ask now than to submit an application that has to be declined
and filed afresh. Any guidance you can offer would be much appreciated.

Thank you for your time.

Yours faithfully,
