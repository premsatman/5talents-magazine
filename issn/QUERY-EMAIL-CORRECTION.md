# Correction to the pre-application query

**Send this as a reply on your own sent message**, so it threads under the
original and they read the two together.

Send it now rather than waiting for their reply. Two reasons. Their guidelines
reserve the right to decline where misleading information has been provided by
the requestor — a correction volunteered before anyone notices reads as care;
the same correction extracted later reads as something else. And the fact you
are correcting is genuinely in your favour: it removes any argument about the
starting year, because a publication that was never publicly issued before 2026
can only have begun its online format in 2026.

Do not apologise twice or explain at length. State what was wrong, state what is
true, say what you are doing about it, and leave the original questions standing.

---

**Subject:** Re: Editorial board requirement for a general-interest magazine — query before applying

---

Dear Sir/Madam,

I must correct one statement in my message below, before it goes any further.

I wrote that 5Talents Magazine was published in print between July 2012 and July
2014. That is not accurate, and I would rather say so now than let it stand.

What is accurate: the magazine was founded in Hyderabad in July 2012, and
eighteen issues were produced between then and July 2014. They were never
printed. They existed only as PDF files, and they were circulated privately to a
small closed list of friends and supporters. They were not sold, not publicly
distributed and not available to any general reader.

The magazine was therefore not publicly published at all until 2026, when it
returned as a website. The starting year on our application will accordingly be
**2026**, being the year the online format began, and the eighteen earlier issues
will be presented on the site as an archive of the magazine's origins rather than
as prior publication. I am updating the website this week so that its wording
matches this in every place it appears.

My two questions in the message below still stand, and I would be grateful for
your guidance on them.

Thank you for your patience with the correction.

Yours faithfully,

Premasis Satman
Publisher, 5Talents Magazine
Hyderabad, Telangana
