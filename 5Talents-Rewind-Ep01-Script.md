## [1. SCREEN TIME – The Chosen wraps filming]

*Rewind karo…*

Pehli khabar, aur emotional wali. *The Chosen* ki shooting officially khatam. 24 September ko. Aath saal, saat seasons, 528 din set pe.

Jonathan Roumie, hamare screen wale Yeshu, ne Instagram pe likha: "The hour has finally come."

Ab mazedaar baat: inhone Season 7 shoot kar liya, jo resurrection ke baare mein hai, aur hum abhi tak crucifixion wala season bhi nahi dekh paaye. Waise spoiler alert: He rises. Sorry, aapne 2000 saal pehle hi suna hoga.

Season 6 Prime Video pe 15 November ko aa raha hai. India mein? The Chosen app pe free hai. Aur churches Episode 1 apne building mein free screen kar sakti hain, 15 November tak. Pastor uncle ko forward kar do.

## [2. REVIVAL REPORT – 882 baptisms in one Sunday]

*Rewind karo…*

Dallas ke paas Lakepointe Church mein ek Sunday ko 882 logon ne baptisma liya. Hamare yahan Sunday ko 82 log aa jaayein toh pastor ji khushi se BP check karwa lete hain.

North Carolina mein Biltmore Church mein ek lake pe 458 logon ne baptisma liya, unki 137 saal ki history mein sabse zyada.

Ab debate: kya itni jaldi baptisma lena theek hai? Na class, na interview, seedha paani mein. Pastor Josh Howerton ne kaha: "New Testament mein har baptisma spontaneous tha." Aur sach mein, Acts 8 mein Philip ne Ethiopian official ko "pehle yeh workbook complete karo" nahi bola.

Lekin asli sawaal Monday ka hai. Sunday ki photo easy hai. February mein in 882 logon ke saath baithne wala koi hai ya nahi? Wahi decide karega ki yeh revival hai ya bas ek bahut accha Sunday.

## [3. CAMPUS SCENE – UniteUS at Miami]

*Rewind karo…*

Isi wave mein campuses bhi hain. University of Miami ke arena mein UniteUS ka event hua, aur service ke baad students bahar water troughs mein line laga ke baptisma liya. Haan, wahi bade tubs jo farms pe hote hain. Bhagwan ko sirf dil chahiye, venue ki koi demand nahi.

Founder Tonya Prewett ka kehna hai: "Agar God basketball arena bhar sakta hai, toh football stadium bhi bhar sakta hai."

Ek reality check bhi: 2023 mein Auburn mein 5,000 log arena mein the, 200 ne baptisma liya. Crowd aur commitment, do alag cheezein hain.

Agle stops: South Alabama 27 October, Belmont Nashville 10 November.

## [4. GAANA BAJANA – Dove Awards 2026]

*Rewind karo…*

57th Dove Awards, Nashville, 6 October. Song of the Year: Josiah Queen ka **"Dusty Bibles"**.

Josiah ne bataya ki unhone yeh gaana ek ghanta doomscroll karne ke baad likha. Unka ek ghanta doomscroll: Gold record. Mera ek ghanta doomscroll: neck pain aur 47 reels about air fryers.

Lekin socho, worship industry ne sabse bada award ek aise gaane ko diya jo bolta hai: "bhai, Bible pe dhool jam gayi hai, phone full charge hai." Yeh trophy nahi, yeh mirror hai.

Baaki winners: Phil Wickham Artist of the Year, Brandon Lake Songwriter of the Year, aur Tasha Cobbs Leonard ka John Legend ke saath "Church" bhi jeeta. Animated film *David* ko Feature Film of the Year mila.

## [5. PURANI BAAT, NAYI BAAT – Faroese New Testament & Tyndale 500]

*Rewind karo…*

Faroe Islands, Scotland aur Iceland ke beech 18 islands, sirf 55,000 log. Itne log toh Gachibowli ke ek IT park mein ek shift mein hote hain. Aur unke liye ek nayi New Testament translation aayi hai, 1937 ke baad pehli baar.

Aur isi saal English Bible ke 500 saal bhi pure hue. William Tyndale ne 1526 mein pehli printed English New Testament banayi. Usse chhupke abroad chhapna pada, kyunki England mein karta toh maar diya jaata. Unhone baad mein maar bhi diya. 3,000 copies chhapi, zyaadatar jala di gayi, aaj sirf 3 bachi hain.

Moral: aaj hum Bible free app pe padh sakte hain. Kam se kam "Dusty Bibles" wala scene toh mat banao.

## [6. HYDERABAD CORNER – Oneness Season 5]

*Rewind karo…*

Ab apne shehar ki baat. 16 October ko, Kompally ke NLAG mein *Oneness* Season 5 hua: 25 singers, 6 languages, Hindi, Telugu, English, Malayalam, Kannada aur Tamil, ek hi stage pe.

Itni languages ek saath sirf do jagah sunne milti hain: Oneness mein aur Hyderabad ke auto stand pe.

Season 1 ke 13.8 million views hain. Concept simple hai: gaana samajh na aaye, lekin jiske baare mein hai woh samajh aata hai.
