Follow these steps to more fully understand your part in the Body of Christ:

•   Realize that you have at least one spiritual gift, probably more (Romans)
•   Pray that God will make your gifts know to you
•   Determine which of your activities the Lord seems to bless and inquire of
    other mature Christians who know you well what your spiritual gifts might be.
•   List here what you believe your spiritual gifts are:

Seek to develop your gifts in the power of the Holy Spirit. Realize that you may
have other gifts of which you are not presently aware, so excercise various gifts.
Be aware that you are accountable to God for stewardship of your spiritual gifts.
