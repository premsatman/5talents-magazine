                                   Paul has been a farmer since he was a young
                                   boy, working his family farm in Ontario,
                                   Canada with his brothers and father until
                                   1997, when he purchased a 38 hectare farm,
                                   just one mile east of his dad’s. Paul accepted
                                   Jesus Christ as His Lord in 1995 and has
                                   never been the same. Paul and his wife,
                                   Jenny have combined their unique talents of
                                   farming and writing to share this story:

                                   When I was a kid and I walked our family
                                   farm, I enjoyed being on the fields. It was fun
                                   to watch things grow. As a kid, I could see
                                   God in everything. I didn’t understand who
                                   God was but I knew God was there. When
                                   things didn’t work the way we expected, like
                                   poor crops or bad weather or animals dying,
                                   I just thought that was the way farming is.
                                   Now I know differently. There are principles
                                   to farming which God has set out in the
                                   Bible which brings success. In my journey as
                                   a farmer, I have slowly learnt some of these
                                   principles, which I call farming, God style.
