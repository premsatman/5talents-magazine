## Caption

They shot Babylon in India. India still can't watch it.

Daniel and the Fiery Furnace opened in America last week. Mena Massoud — yes, Aladdin — plays Daniel. Much of it was filmed in India, including the throne-room scene in an amphitheatre there.

Massoud is Coptic. His family came to Canada from Egypt when he was three and a half, and he says the church was where his childhood happened: "I grew up doing church plays and Stations of the Cross in elementary school."

He reads the book as an immigrant story. Daniel and his friends were taken from Judah to Babylon, given new names, new food, new schooling — and tested. Anyone who has been the only one in the room who prays differently knows that room.

The directors say something happened while they were filming Daniel 2. As Massoud spoke the lines about a kingdom that lasts forever, the wind came up, tents blew across the set and the thunder rolled in. Their words, their reading of it. They kept the cameras rolling.

It opened at $3.3 million across 1,525 theatres — sixth place that week. The New York Times said it "barely manages to reach room temperature". Audiences gave it 96%.

And there is still no Indian release date. Made here. Not showing here.
