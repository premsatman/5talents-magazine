## Instagram caption

The Chosen has filmed its last scene.

Filming ended on Thursday 24 September near Dallas, Texas, after eight years, seven seasons and 528
days on set.

Jonathan Roumie, who has played Jesus since 2017, posted four words first: "the hour has finally
come." Then he quoted Paul. "I have fought the good fight; I have finished the race; I have kept the
faith."

Giavani Cairo, who plays Thaddeus, filmed himself taking off the costume and sandals for the last
time. Austin Reed Alleman posted his first day on set, 13 October 2020, beside his last, 24 September
2026.

At the wrap party Dallas Jenkins said his eyes were teary from the crying he had been doing that
night, and that he will never fully understand why he was the one chosen to make it.

It began as a crowdfunded pilot with no distribution deal. More than 300 million people have watched
it since.

Season 6, the crucifixion, reaches Prime Video on 15 November. Season 7, the resurrection, arrives in
2028.

The cast have already said goodbye to a story the rest of us are still in the middle of.

Which scene are you waiting for? Tell us in the comments.

## Editorial notes
- Wrap DATE: outlets printed Thursday 25 September and Thursday 26 September. Both are wrong on the
  calendar: 25 Sep 2026 was a Friday and 26 Sep was a Saturday. They all agree on THURSDAY, and the
  only Thursday that week was 24 September, which also matches Alleman's own dated post. We print
  Thursday 24 September.
- One outlet's summary also placed the wrap at Camp Hoblitzelle in late April. That does not fit the
  September farewell posts, so we left it out rather than guess.
- This is NOT breaking news. The wrap was ten days ago and the official account has already moved on
  to Season 6 clip promotion. The piece is framed as the goodbye plus what is still coming, which is
  why it ends on the 15 November date rather than the wrap.
- All artwork is ours (tools/chosen_cards.py). We hold no licensed photography of the cast, and the
  farewell posts are described rather than reproduced.
