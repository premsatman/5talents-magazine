Can you follow Jesus and keep your culture?

Angh, a Konyak-language film from Nagaland, just became the first Indian film to win the Platform Prize at the Toronto International Film Festival. That makes it eligible for the 2027 Oscars.

It's set in 1962. A Konyak chief and his son are the last holdouts in their village as a road, a school and an American Baptist missionary arrive. Critics say the film grieves what was lost, without turning the missionary into a simple villain.

Nagaland is about 88% Christian today. So the film leaves Indian believers with a real question: when the gospel came, did it have to come with a necktie and an English hymnbook?
