## Why the kit graphics are on hold

The hub licence grants "a non-exclusive, limited, and non-transferable license to show The Chosen for **non-commercial purposes in your Church or Ministry**", and adds that channels "featuring content from The Chosen may not be monetized."

5talentsmag.com runs ad slots and Travelpayouts, so we're a monetised publisher, and a magazine isn't what the licence has in mind by "your Church or Ministry". The graphics exist to promote screenings, which is what our article does too, so a yes is likely. We should still get it in writing before using them. Record the reply in `rightsNote`, for example "Email from Come and See press, 22 Sep 2026".

## Rules that apply every day (standing)

1. **Credit is not permission.** Every image needs one of the `rightsBasis` values, and "found it online" doesn't count as one.
2. **Newsroom and studio press images** (press.amazonmgmstudios.com, Netflix Tudum press, network press sites) are released for editorial coverage of that title. That covers the website. On Instagram, still prefer our own cards.
3. **Church/ministry kits** (Come and See, Angel Studios guild kits, church promo packs) are licensed for churches, so ask before using them.
4. **Getty, AP, Reuters, and athletes' own Instagram photos:** never, unless we have a licence. For sports prayer moments, embed the original post (Instagram/X embed), which keeps the image on their servers.
5. **When in doubt, use the typographic card.** It's our house style.
