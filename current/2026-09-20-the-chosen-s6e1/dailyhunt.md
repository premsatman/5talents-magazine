**Title:** The Chosen Season 6 is premiering in churches first. Here's how to watch from India

**Image:** hero-1600x900.png (ours). Don't use the Come and See kit graphic.

---

The Chosen, the Jesus series watched by more than 300 million people, is doing something unusual with Season 6. Episode 1 isn't opening on a streaming app. It's showing in churches.

Since 15 September, churches and parishes have been able to screen the episode free, two months before the public release. They can't sell tickets, download it or livestream it, so people have to watch it together in person.

The 55-minute episode starts on the night Jesus is arrested. The disciples are frightened and scattered after Judas' betrayal, and Nicodemus confronts the high priest in front of the council. The whole season covers about 24 hours, the day of the crucifixion.

"People are going to have questions," creator Dallas Jenkins told ChurchLeaders. That's why he wanted the first episode seen in church.

**Watching from India:** Amazon's list of Prime Video regions for Season 6 (15 November 2026) doesn't include India. Seasons 1 to 5 are on The Chosen app, so you can catch up there. For Episode 1, ask your pastor to register the church at comeandseefoundation.org/church.

The finale will be released in cinemas in spring 2027.
