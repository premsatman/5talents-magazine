Husband. Boss. Pastor. In Mica Miller's life, all three were the same man.

Death of the Pastor's Wife is a three-part Netflix docuseries about Mica, a 30-year-old worship leader in Myrtle Beach, South Carolina, who died in April 2024. She married her church's pastor, worked for his church, and by 2024 she was trying to leave.

Her husband was later indicted on federal cyberstalking charges. He pleaded not guilty and denies them. Nothing has been proven in court.

The hardest question in the series isn't really about him. It's about everyone around him: the board that never said no, and the church that went quiet.
