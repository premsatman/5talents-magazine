## 🎥 [2. BIBLE APP — Psalm 91] — shoot today

*Rewind karo…*

Aapke phone mein Bible App hai? Agar hai, toh aap akele nahi hain. Is saal har din lagbhag 1 crore 85 lakh log yeh app kholte hain. Pichhle saal se 13 percent zyada.

Ab interesting sawaal yeh hai: log is app mein sabse zyada kya search karte hain? Company ne bataya hai ki is saal sabse zyada search hua Psalm 91. Woh psalm jo shuru hota hai, "Jo Parmeshwar ki chhaya mein rehta hai, woh surakshit rahega." Yaani ek chhupne ki jagah, ek suraksha ki jagah ke baare mein.

Aur uske saath jo shabd sabse zyada search hue, woh the anxiety, hope aur healing. Yaani chinta, ummeed aur changai.

Socho, search box mein koi dikhaave ke liye kuch type nahi karta. Hum wahi type karte hain jiski humein us waqt sach mein zaroorat hoti hai. Aur duniya bhar ke log sabse zyada suraksha aur shanti dhoondh rahe hain.

Ab ek kaam ki baat. November ka poora mahina "Global Bible Month" hai. Iska challenge simple hai: tees din, roz Bible kholo. App se bhi kar sakte ho, aur ghar wali chhapi hui Bible se bhi. Website globalbiblemonth.com hai, aur yeh Hindi, Telugu, Tamil aur Malayalam mein bhi available hai. Haan, Bengali, Marathi aur Kannada abhi website pe nahi hain. Lekin challenge ke liye kisi website ki zaroorat nahi hai. Bas roz Bible kholni hai.

## 🎥 [4. FORREST FRANK — tour mein sermon] — shoot today

*Rewind karo…*

Agar aap Instagram Reels dekhte ho, toh aapne Forrest Frank ka koi na koi gaana zaroor suna hoga. Woh America ke ek Christian singer hain, aur aaj Christian music ke sabse bade naamon mein se ek hain.

Pehle woh ek normal pop band mein the. Phir unhone sab chhod ke sirf Christian music banana shuru kiya. Aur career chhota hone ke bajaye aur bada ho gaya. Unke gaane 400 crore se zyada baar stream ho chuke hain.

Is saal unhone ek tour kiya, "The Jesus Generation Tour". Tickets bikne shuru hue, aur sirf das din mein saade chaar lakh se zyada tickets bik gaye. Unnatis shehron mein stadium bhar gaye. Ab is tour pe 2027 mein ek film bhi aa rahi hai.

Lekin asli khabar yeh hai. Har raat, concert ke beech mein, music ruk jaata tha. Aur stage pe ek pastor aate the, JP Pokluda. Woh poore stadium ke saamne gospel preach karte the aur logon ko Yeshu pe vishwas karne ke liye invite karte the. Har raat. Itne bade show ke beech mein ek proper sermon.

Aur Forrest ko iski keemat bhi chukani padi. Pichhle saal unhone Yeshu ke wapas aane ke baare mein ek video daala. Video ko das lakh se zyada likes mile, lekin unke hisaab se lagbhag tees hazaar followers unhe chhod ke chale gaye. Phir bhi unhone apni baat nahi badli.

Ab ek baat hamare liye. Forrest Frank kabhi India nahi aaye, phir bhi unke gaane hamare phones tak pahunch gaye, Reels ke through. Wahi Reels, wahi upload button, hamare har worship leader ke phone mein bhi hai. Toh sawaal yeh hai: hamare Hindi, Telugu aur Mizo worship gaane duniya tak kab pahunchenge?

## 🎥 [5. SOUND BARRIER — kya hua] — shoot today

*Rewind karo…*

Episode 1 mein maine aapko Sound Barrier conference ke baare mein bataya tha. Woh 31 October ko Hyderabad ke Gachibowli Indoor Stadium mein hua, aur main bhi wahan tha. Toh chaliye batata hoon kya hua.

Organisers ke hisaab se din bhar mein teen hazaar se zyada log aaye.

Aur jaisa maine bataya tha, stadium ke andar jaane se pehle bahar ek job fair tha. Saath se zyada companies thi, jaise Tech Mahindra, Concentrix, Foxconn aur IDBI Bank. Walk-in interview tha, aur job dhoondhne walon se ek rupaya bhi nahi liya gaya. Aur suniye, subah das baje tak lagbhag pachaas logon ke haath mein offer letter aa chuka tha. Spot pe. Conference shuru hone se pehle hi.

Lunch ke time pe ek alag session tha, jismein police officers, ek government advocate, software engineers aur marketing professionals se seedha baat kar sakte the. Yaani ek naujawan subah job interview de, aur dopahar ko us field ke kisi senior se mil le.

Stage pe ek speaker, Justin Owens, ne ek simple baat boli jo mujhe yaad reh gayi: jo aap sunte ho, wahi aap maanne lagte ho. Jo maante ho, wahi bolte ho. Jo bolte ho, wahi baar baar karte ho. Aur jo baar baar karte ho, wahi aap ban jaate ho.

Aur founder Reinhard Komanapalli ne bataya ki unhone is conference ka idea 2012 mein ek kaagaz pe likha tha, aur 2024 tak kuch nahi kiya. Barah saal. Toh agar aapka sapna abhi bhi kaagaz pe hai, ghabraiye mat. Kabhi kabhi time lagta hai.

## 🎥 [6. KOREA — Gen Z aur church] — shoot today

*Rewind karo…*

South Korea ka naam suna hai na? Duniya ka sabse bada megachurch wahin hai. Ek time pe America ke baad sabse zyada missionaries Korea se hi bheje jaate the.

Lekin aaj wahan ek badi problem hai. Bees se tees saal ke chaar mein se teen Korean log kisi bhi dharm ko nahi maante. Aur teenagers mein yeh number aur bhi zyada hai.

Toh jo log church chhod ke gaye, unse poocha gaya: kyun chhoda? Sabse badi wajah yeh nikli ki 74 percent logon ke paas church mein koi aisa insaan hi nahi tha jisse woh apni problem share kar sakein.

Ab kuch nayi churches yeh badal rahi hain. Seoul mein ek church cafe jaisi building ke basement mein hoti hai. Pastor T-shirt mein preach karte hain aur beech mein logon ke sawaalon ka jawab dete hain. Small groups ko "crew" bolte hain, jo hafte mein saath football khelte hain.

Lekin yeh churches saaf kehti hain ki asli baat design ya lighting ki nahi hai. Ek pastor ne kaha: "Form nahi, essence. Structure nahi, relationship. Rules nahi, gospel."

Ek tees saal ki ladki ne bahut sacchi baat boli. Uski purani church itni badi thi ki logon ko umar ke hisaab se alag alag groups mein baant diya jaata tha. Aur isi wajah se use kabhi koi bada, experienced Christian mila hi nahi jo use guide kar sake.

Aur America mein bhi research yahi keh rahi hai. Gen Z church kyun aa rahi hai? Pehli wajah: prayer. Doosri: doosron ke saath milke worship karna. Koi bada program nahi, simple cheezein.

Toh hamare liye seekh yeh hai. India mein zyada tar churches chhoti hain, chalees pachaas log, jahan bacche, jawaan aur buzurg ek hi kamre mein baithte hain. Yeh koi kamzori nahi hai. Yeh wahi cheez hai jo badi churches ab wapas dhoondh rahi hain. Bas ek kaam karo: kisi naujawan ke saath baitho, aur use apni kahani batao.
