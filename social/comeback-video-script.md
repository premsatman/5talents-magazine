# 5Talents Magazine — 30-second comeback video

Screen recording of www.5talentsmag.com. Voiceover plus on-screen text.
Target length 0:30. Voiceover is ~80 words — read it unhurried, do not rush to fit.

---

## Voiceover (read straight through)

In 2012, we started a magazine for young Christians in India.

Issue after issue. Four hundred pages.

Then in 2014, we stopped.

Screens got smaller. Our pages didn't.

Twelve years later, all of it is back. Every article. Every Talent Search interview.

Finally readable on the phone in your hand.

5Talents Magazine. We're back.

## Production notes

**Record the flipbook separately.** Flipbooks are slow to load and a three-second
spinner in the middle of a 30-second cut will lose the viewer. Shoot the page-turn
on its own and edit it in.

**Use 12 years, not 14.** The magazine began July 2012 and stopped July 2014.
The brand is 14 years old; the silence was 12 years. For a comeback, the silence
is the number. Save "14 years ago we started" for a founding-anniversary post.

**Alternate opening** if the hook needs to be harder — the first two seconds
decide whether anyone stays. Open cold on the `/archive` cover grid with no
narration, then: *"These disappeared for twelve years."* Then cut to the home page.

**Capture settings.** Record at 1080x1920 portrait for Facebook and Instagram feed
and Reels. If capturing a desktop browser, record at 1920x1080 and crop, or narrow
the browser window to a phone width first so the mobile layout is what shows —
that matches the "readable on the phone in your hand" line.
